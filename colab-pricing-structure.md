## WHAT THE MARKET CHARGES

**Custom real estate CRM builds**
Market rate for a real estate CRM MVP with lead management, transaction tracking, and email campaigns: $8,000 to $16,000, 3 to 5 week build timeline. The cheapest fixed price competitor found (Webscension) charges $2,997 for the same scope.

**Done-for-you CRM implementation**
For a small to growing real estate team on a platform like Follow Up Boss or Lofty, implementation lands between a few thousand and the low five figures all in. Industry advice is that under five agents you DIY, above five you pay for done-for-you.

**AI automation builds**
Simple automation, one to two workflows: $500 to $1,500
Medium project, three to five integrated workflows: $1,500 to $4,000
Full stack across sales, marketing, ops, reporting: $4,000 to $10,000
Growth system with dashboards and QA: $4,000 to $12,000

**Monthly retainers**
AI automation support and optimization: $500 to $2,000/mo at the SMB level
Median small to mid market retainer: $2,800 to $7,000/mo
Broker-specific ongoing workflow support: $500 to $5,000/mo

**What clients pay instead of you (per seat, forever)**
Generic CRM (Pipedrive, HubSpot): $12 to $50 per seat per month
Real estate CRM (Follow Up Boss, LionDesk): $40 to $100 per seat per month
Brivity: starts around $449/mo
Add SMS, call recording, and integrations and a $50/mo platform becomes $200+/mo

## THE NEW STRUCTURE

### Free: Discovery Call
30 minutes. No charge. No deck.
This is the funnel entry and the qualifier. Never charge for this.

### Tier 1: Spark — $997 one time
One tool, one workflow, built and delivered.
For: a single specific problem. Lead capture to follow up. One automation.
Delivered in days.
Market position: mid range of the $500 to $1,500 simple automation band.

### Tier 2: System — $2,997 one time
Full custom CRM or multi agent build. Single user or very small team.
For: replacing spreadsheets and disconnected tools with one system.
Includes database, dashboard, and up to three connected workflows.
Market position: matches the cheapest fixed price competitor, at one third of the $8,000 to $16,000 market rate, delivered in days instead of 3 to 5 weeks.

### Tier 3: Team — $5,997 one time
Multi user build with role separation. Agents, TC, admin, manager views.
For: a team where different people need different access and different views.
Includes everything in System plus user roles, permissions, and team reporting.
Market position: still well under the $8,000 to $16,000 market rate and under most done-for-you implementations.
Note: brokerage-wide rollouts quoted separately, typically $8,000 to $15,000.

### Ongoing: Edge — $497/mo solo, $997/mo team
Support, fixes, updates, and new automations as the business grows.
Cancel anytime, 30 days notice, no contract lock.
Market position: bottom of the $500 to $2,000 SMB retainer band on purpose. This is a relationship price, not a max-extraction price.

## THE PITCH MATH FOR A REAL ESTATE TEAM

A four person team on Follow Up Boss at $60 per seat pays $2,880 a year, every year, forever, for a platform they did not design.

System at $2,997 is a one time cost. Year two they pay nothing unless they want Edge. Year three the platform user has paid $8,640 and still does not own anything.

That is the close. You are not the cheap option. You are the one they stop paying for.

## RULES

1. Never quote a range on a call. Quote the tier.
2. Discovery is always free. If it feels like work, it is scope, and scope is a build.
3. Add ons are published prices. Do not calculate live.
4. If someone pushes on price, remove scope, do not discount. Move them to Spark.
5. Do not pitch Edge on call one. Plant it. "Once it is built we usually do a monthly check in to keep it running and add things as you grow. Optional, we will figure that out after."
6. Raise prices when you are booked out two weeks. Not before.
