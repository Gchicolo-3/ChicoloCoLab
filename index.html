Origin Story

I built the first one
for myself.

My name is George Chicolo. I run a real estate practice in Hillsdale, New Jersey with my wife Christine.

A few years ago I was drowning. Leads slipping through, follow ups I meant to send and never did, admin work eating the hours I should have been selling in. Nothing was connected and everything depended on me remembering it.

So I built our own system. Lead tracking, pipeline stages, follow up sequences, transaction activity, all in one place, built around how we actually work instead of how a platform thought we should.

It worked. Then a coffee supplier asked if I could do something for his accounts. Then a contractor running his whole business off sticky notes. Then a commercial consultant who needed prospecting to happen without him sitting there doing it.

Different industries, same problem every time. The work is not the bottleneck. The gaps between the work are.
